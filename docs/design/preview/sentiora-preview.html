How it works

From a page you read to an answer you trust.

  1. You read like normalSentiora saves the page in the background while you're signed in.
  2. It becomes a memoryClean text, the source, and a few tags. Ads and menus are thrown away.
  3. Find it by meaningSearch the idea, not the title. The right page comes back even if you forgot every word.
  4. Ask, with sourcesAnswers come only from what you saved, each claim linked to its page.

Does it read my browsing history?

No. It saves pages as you visit them while you're signed in, never your past history. Sensitive sites are skipped, and you can block sites or delete memories anytime.

Can I save PDFs that are on my computer?

Yes. Open them in Chrome or upload them from the app.
